# TalentOS AI

### AI-Powered Resume Screening & Candidate Ranking System

TalentOS AI is an intelligent hiring platform designed to help recruiters and HR teams screen large volumes of resumes efficiently and make faster, fairer, and data-driven hiring decisions.

Unlike traditional Applicant Tracking Systems that rely heavily on keyword matching, TalentOS AI uses semantic AI to understand the context of job descriptions and candidate resumes, evaluate candidate suitability, and generate transparent rankings with explainable insights.

---

## Problem

Recruiters often need to manually review hundreds of resumes for a single job opening. Traditional resume screening systems primarily depend on keyword matching, which can overlook qualified candidates whose skills or experience are described differently.

The hiring process can therefore become:

- Time-consuming
- Difficult to scale
- Dependent on keyword matching
- Subjective in candidate ranking
- Difficult to explain
- Vulnerable to demographic bias

TalentOS AI addresses these challenges through intelligent resume understanding, semantic matching, bias-aware evaluation, and explainable candidate ranking.

---

## Solution

TalentOS AI provides a single AI-powered hiring intelligence system that:

1. Understands the Job Description
2. Parses and understands candidate resumes
3. Performs semantic candidate-to-job matching
4. Evaluates candidates using job-relevant evidence
5. Applies bias-aware evaluation
6. Calculates candidate suitability scores
7. Generates transparent candidate rankings
8. Assists recruiters with personalized interview questions

### Core Solution

> **TalentOS AI helps recruiters identify the most suitable candidates quickly, accurately, and fairly by understanding job and resume context rather than relying only on keywords.**

---

## Key Features

### 1. Resume Understanding
Extracts and structures relevant information from candidate resumes, including:

- Skills
- Education
- Experience
- Projects
- Certifications
- Achievements

### 2. Job Description Understanding
Analyzes the job description to identify:

- Required skills
- Preferred skills
- Experience requirements
- Educational requirements
- Role responsibilities

### 3. Semantic Candidate Matching
Uses semantic similarity to compare candidates with the job requirements beyond exact keyword matching.

### 4. Bias-Aware Evaluation
Focuses candidate evaluation on job-relevant qualifications while reducing the influence of demographic attributes.

### 5. Candidate Suitability Scoring
Generates a structured score representing how well a candidate matches the requirements of a particular role.

### 6. Explainable Candidate Ranking
Provides recruiters with understandable reasons behind candidate rankings, including:

- Matching strengths
- Relevant experience
- Skill gaps
- Project relevance
- Overall suitability

### 7. AI Interview Assistant
Generates role-specific interview questions based on the job requirements and candidate profile.

### 8. Recruiter Dashboard
Provides recruiters with a centralized view of:

- Ranked candidates
- Match scores
- AI insights
- Hiring recommendations
- Interview questions
